Court Legends 2.5D

Arcade energy + simulation-inspired basketball systems

Controls

  • Move: WASD / Arrows
  • Sprint: Shift
  • Dribble Move: J (crossover / spin / behind-back)
  • Pass: U (chest) / I (bounce) / O (alley-oop)
  • Shoot: K (tap = layup, hold = jumper)
  • Dunk / Block: Space
  • Steal / Contest: L
  • Replay: R
